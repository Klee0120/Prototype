ServiceWorks Time Tracker -- Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

ServiceWorks Time Tracker is a browser extension used internally by Cushman & Wakefield Toyota Operations & Financial Management admin staff to log their own work time against ServiceWorks (services-works.com). This page explains what the extension does with data.

What the extension accesses

The extension communicates only with services-works.com. When you log in, it sends your employee ID and PIN to that server to authenticate, the same way logging in on the ServiceWorks website does. It does not read, collect, or transmit data from any other website or tab.

What is stored, and where

  • A session token and your name/role/ID are stored in the browser's local extension storage (chrome.storage.local) so you stay logged in between uses. This data stays on your device and is never sent anywhere except back to services-works.com to authenticate your requests.
  • Time-log entries you create (category, duration, notes, and any PO or technician you link) are stored in the ServiceWorks application database, exactly as they would be if entered on the website itself.

What the extension does not do

  • It does not use analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking of any kind.
  • It does not sell or share data with any third party.
  • It does not access your browsing history, other tabs, or any website other than services-works.com.

Removing your data

Uninstalling the extension clears everything stored locally in your browser. To remove time-log entries themselves, contact your ServiceWorks administrator.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to your ServiceWorks administrator.
